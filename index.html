Botox

Botox is the treatment most people ask about first, and it is the one where a light hand matters most. It works by relaxing the small muscles that create lines when you frown, squint, or raise your brows. A few tiny injections, a few minutes, and the lines soften over the next several days. It does not freeze your face when it is done well. You can still smile, still raise your brows, and still look like yourself. The effect lasts about three to four months, and most people come back when they notice the lines returning. The team starts with a conservative amount, because you can always add more, and they will check in after two weeks to see how it settled.

Lip Filler

Lips are the treatment where a med spa's taste shows. A small amount of filler, placed carefully, can restore volume that has faded with age, define the border of the lip, or balance lips that are uneven. The goal is not a dramatic change. It is lips that look like yours, only rested and hydrated. The team uses a careful technique, talks through how much to use, and will stop before the result looks overdone. Swelling for a day or two is normal, and the result settles over the next week. If you want a subtle change, say so. The best compliment after lip filler is that nobody can tell what changed, only that you look well.

Facial Balancing

Facial balancing is the art of looking at the whole face, not one feature at a time. A little filler in the cheeks can lift the lower face. A small amount in the chin can balance a profile. The team looks at how your features work together, and they talk about proportion before they talk about syringes. It is not about adding volume everywhere. It is about placing a small amount in the right spot so the face looks rested and in harmony. Many people who ask for one thing, like fuller lips, find that a small adjustment elsewhere gives them the result they actually wanted. That is the value of a consultation that looks at the whole picture.

Microneedling

Microneedling is a treatment for skin texture, not for lines alone. Tiny needles create small, controlled openings in the skin, which prompts the skin to make new collagen. Over a few weeks, the skin looks smoother, pores look smaller, and acne scars look softer. It is a good option for people who want to improve their skin without injections, and it pairs well with other treatments. There is some redness for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, and the team will tell you how to care for your skin afterward. A series of treatments usually gives the best result, and the team will be honest about how many you are likely to need.

Cheek Filler

Cheeks lose volume as we age, and that loss shows up as deeper lines around the mouth and a tired look in the mid-face. Cheek filler restores that volume, which lifts the skin gently and softens the lines below. It is one of the most natural-looking treatments when it is done with a light hand, because it works with the structure of your face rather than against it. The result lasts a year or more, and the team will place it carefully, checking the balance from every angle. If you have ever looked in the mirror and thought your face looked tired even when you felt fine, cheek filler is often the treatment that explains why, and fixes it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Botox hurt?

Most people feel a small pinch that lasts a second. The needles are very fine, and the treatment takes only a few minutes. If you are nervous, tell the team, and they will go slowly and talk you through each step.

How long does Botox last?

About three to four months for most people. The effect builds over a few days, peaks at about two weeks, and then fades gradually. Most clients book their next visit when they notice the lines returning.

Will I look frozen after Botox?

Not when it is done well. A conservative dose softens the lines while letting you move your face normally. The team starts small, and you can always add more at a follow-up if you want a stronger effect.

How long does lip filler last?

Usually six to twelve months, depending on the product and how your body breaks it down. The team will tell you what to expect and when to book a touch-up.

Is there downtime after a Diamond Glow Facial?

Almost none. Your skin may look a little flushed for an hour or two, and then it settles. You can go back to work or out for the evening the same day.

Can I get Botox and a facial on the same day?

Sometimes, but not always. The team will tell you the best order for your treatments, because some combinations work better on different days. Ask when you book, and they will plan the visit for you.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Book a consultation. The team will look at your skin, listen to what bothers you, and recommend the treatment that fits, with a written price. If nothing is needed yet, they will say so.
